BillableExpenses.lines(book, period, currency) returns one LineItem per billable expense whose date the period contains, in the book's order, with the payee as the description, quantity 1 and the amount as Money of the same cents in the given currency
The lines BillableExpenses.lines returns cannot be modified
BillableExpenses.invoice(number, period, currency, book) returns a new Invoice with that number, period and currency, holding those lines
