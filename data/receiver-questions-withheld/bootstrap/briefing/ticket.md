- The capability (one invariant, one home --: (1) every stagnation detector REPORTS a typed stuck-observation (signature, site, count, first/last cycle) to one component instead of carrying its own reaction; (2) that component applies an ORDERED ladder -- say it concretely -> change strategy (diverge / replan / divide) -> rewind to the last good checkpoint and forbid the failed path -> halt; (3) a LEDGER keyed by signature records which rung was applied and whether the signature recurred after it, and a recurrence moves to the next rung. Deterministic; a model may fill in the CONTENT of a rung (the concrete advice), never choose the rung. Existing reactions become rungs, and the duplicates are deleted, not stacked.
