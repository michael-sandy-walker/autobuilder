  A new project, adopted as it stands. Its tree holds only its build file (pom.xml), a README, and the
  operator's specification under src/test/java; nothing exists under src/main/java yet. In its own words,
  from its README:

    "pettycash -- A petty-cash book: amounts in cents, expenses with a payee, a category and whether they are
    billable, totals by category and period."

  It is a library, in package org.pettycash, with no dependencies beyond the JDK.

  - Amount is a record of an amount in cents (long cents). Amount.parse(String) reads a decimal with at most
    two places and an optional leading '-' ("12.50" is 1250, "0.5" is 50); anything else raises
    IllegalArgumentException. plus(Amount) adds, negate() changes the sign, toString() prints two places:
    "12.50", "-0.05".
  - Period is a record of a first and a last day (LocalDate from, LocalDate to), from not after to, else
    IllegalArgumentException. contains(LocalDate) is true from the first to the last day inclusive; days()
    returns how many days it spans, both ends included (a long).
  - Category is an enum: FOOD, TRAVEL, OFFICE, OTHER, in that order.
  - Expense is a record of a date (LocalDate), a payee (String, not blank), an amount (Amount, positive), a
    category (Category) and whether it is billable (boolean billable), else IllegalArgumentException.
  - ExpenseBook is a class with a no-argument constructor. add(Expense) adds an expense at the end;
    expenses() returns them in that order, as a List that cannot be modified; total() is the sum of their
    amounts and totalFor(Category) the sum of one category's (zero when there are none); within(Period)
    lists the expenses whose date the period contains, and billable() the billable ones, both in order.

  The specification is PettyCashSpecTest in src/test/java: its tests say exactly what the library must
  return, and they fail until the code provides it.

  OBJECTIVE: every test in PettyCashSpecTest passes, and the whole suite passes.

  The classes to change are Amount, Period, Category, Expense and ExpenseBook -- none exists yet: create them
  under src/main/java in package org.pettycash. DO NOT modify anything under src/test: the specification is
  the operator's, and if one of its tests fails, the code is what is wrong.
