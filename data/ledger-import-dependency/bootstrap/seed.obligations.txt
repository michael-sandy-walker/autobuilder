LedgerImport.importLines(ledger, lines) appends one Entry per accepted line, in order, and returns an ImportReport of the number imported and the rejected lines in line order
A line is read with linetokens' LineTokenizer.tokenize, and a line it rejects is rejected with the TokenizeException's own position and message
A blank line is skipped, and lines are numbered from 1 as given, blank ones included
The names are account, amount and memo; any other name rejects the line with "unknown name '<name>'"
amount is a decimal with at most two places and an optional leading '-', and one that is not, or is zero, rejects the line with "invalid amount"
A line without an account or with a blank one is rejected with "missing account", and one without an amount with "missing amount", at position -1
A rejected line appends nothing and does not stop the import
The list of rejected lines cannot be modified

An Entry needs an account that is not null or blank and a non-zero amount, else it raises IllegalArgumentException
An Entry with a null memo has the empty string as its memo
Ledger.append(entry) adds the entry at the end and returns its number counting from 1
Ledger.transfer(from, to, cents, memo) appends from's debit then to's credit and returns the number of the credit
Ledger.transfer raises IllegalArgumentException and appends nothing when cents is not positive or from and to are the same
Ledger.entries() returns every entry in order, in a list that cannot be modified and does not change when more are appended
Ledger.balance(account) returns the sum of that account's amounts, 0 for an account with no entries
