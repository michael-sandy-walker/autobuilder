  A new project, adopted as it stands. Its tree holds only its build file (pom.xml), a README, and the
  operator's specification under src/test/java; nothing exists under src/main/java yet. In its own words,
  from its README:

    "invoicer -- Invoices in one currency: money with its currency, billing periods, invoice lines and their
    total."

  It is a library, in package org.invoicer, with no dependencies beyond the JDK.

  - Money is a record of an amount in cents (long cents) and a currency (String currency): three upper-case
    letters, else IllegalArgumentException.
  - Money.parse(String) reads "<decimal> <currency>": a decimal with at most two places and an optional
    leading '-' ("12.50 EUR" is 1250 cents in EUR, "0.5 CHF" is 50), one space, the currency. Anything else
    raises IllegalArgumentException.
  - plus(Money) adds two amounts of the same currency (another currency raises IllegalArgumentException);
    times(int n) multiplies. toString() prints two places and the currency: "12.50 EUR", "-0.05 USD".
  - Period is a record of a first and a last day (LocalDate from, LocalDate to), from not after to, else
    IllegalArgumentException. contains(LocalDate) is true from the first to the last day inclusive; days()
    returns how many days it spans, both ends included (a long).
  - LineItem is a record of a description (not blank), a quantity (int, positive) and a unit price (Money),
    else IllegalArgumentException; amount() is the unit price times the quantity.
  - Invoice is a class constructed with a number (String), a period (Period) and a currency (String).
    addLine(LineItem) adds a line whose unit price is in the invoice's currency, else raises
    IllegalArgumentException and adds nothing. lines() returns the lines in the order added, as a List that
    cannot be modified. total() is the sum of the lines' amounts, zero in the invoice's currency when there
    are none. number() and period() return what it was constructed with.

  The specification is InvoicerSpecTest in src/test/java: its tests say exactly what the library must
  return, and they fail until the code provides it.

  OBJECTIVE: every test in InvoicerSpecTest passes, and the whole suite passes.

  The classes to change are Money, Period, LineItem and Invoice -- none exists yet: create them under
  src/main/java in package org.invoicer. DO NOT modify anything under src/test: the specification is the
  operator's, and if one of its tests fails, the code is what is wrong.
